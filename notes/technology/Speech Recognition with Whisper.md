# Speech Recognition with Whisper

Whisper is an open speech-to-text model. Sizes range from tiny to large; English-only variants (tiny.en, base.en, small.en) are faster and more accurate for English. faster-whisper runs it efficiently on CPU with int8 weights. Latency matters more than perfection for a voice assistant, so pick the smallest model that hears you reliably. Pairs with [[Text to Speech Basics]] for a full voice loop and benefits from [[Async Programming]].
