# Offline First Design

An offline-first system works with no network and treats the internet as optional. Bundle libraries locally instead of loading from a CDN, store data on disk, bind servers to 127.0.0.1 and pin dependency versions. Verify the claim by logging every outbound connection during a turn. Local models ([[How Local LLMs Work]]) and local speech ([[Speech Recognition with Whisper]]) make it possible.
