# JARVIS Speech Recognition & Translation Pipeline — Stage 1 Codebase Audit Report

## 1. Executive Summary
A full source-code and empirical diagnostic audit of the Jarvis audio, speech-to-text (STT), transcript preservation, translation, API, and frontend pipeline identified **multiple interacting root causes** beyond Whisper itself:

1. **Browser & Stream Capture Loss (`VoiceDock.tsx`, `voice.py`)**: Browser recording (`encodeWav16`) downsamples `44.1/48 kHz` audio to `16 kHz` using nearest-neighbor decimation (`samples[Math.floor(i * ratio)]`) without an anti-aliasing filter. Host capture (`VoiceLoop`) tries `16 kHz mono` instead of preferring native hardware sample rates first, takes only channel `0` (`frame[:, 0]`) instead of downmixing stereo, and resamples isolated 80 ms chunks per block instead of resampling whole utterances.
2. **Missing Audio Preprocessing & Diagnostics (`stt.py`)**: No safe gain staging (recorded real microphone clips in `benchmarks/samples/` peaked at `0.174–0.210`), no DC/high-pass rumble filtering, no clipping/low-volume/silence detection, no short-utterance padding (`"Stop."` 0.59s failed with 100% WER across `base` and `small`), and no raw audio diagnostic report. Critically, empirical testing proved that blind peak normalization amplifies pure silence (`live_mixed.wav`, `peak=0.0027`) ~259× into Whisper repetition loops (`"1-2-3-4-4..."`, `"වවව..."`, `"ༀༀ..."`) with spuriously high `avg_logprob=-0.057`.
3. **Malayalam Script & Phonetic Drift in Generic Whisper (`stt.py`, `benchmarks/live_mic_results.json`)**: On real Malayalam microphone audio (`live_malayalam.wav`), `small` (`auto`) detected `ml` (`0.929`), yet transcribed Malayalam speech into **Devanagari script** (`"विंदान समयः"` instead of Malayalam Unicode) and `base` (`auto`) misclassified it as Tagalog (`tl`, `0.148`). Similarly, `base` (`auto`) misclassified English `live_wake.wav` as Marathi (`mr`, `0.171`). There is currently no `ASRProvider` abstraction, no specialized Malayalam/Indic ASR adapter, no script-mismatch check, and no confidence/quality-triggered retry.
4. **Metadata & Translation Separation Bugs (`api.py`, `assistant.py`, `translator.py`)**:
   - `api.py:309` and `assistant.py:456` read `stt_res.get("language", "unknown")` instead of `"detected_language"`, publishing `"language": "unknown"` on every `assistant.transcript.partial` event.
   - `Translator.process_stt` (`prefer_whisper_for_english=True`) bypasses `corrected_transcript` and alternative ASR results by re-running `stt.transcribe(..., task="translate")` directly on raw audio when translating to English.
   - `Translator.translate_text` and `correct_text_with_llm` lack explicit non-hallucination constraints, code-switching preservation rules, and post-translation semantic verification (numbers, dates, negation, entities, intent).
   - `VoiceTranscriptRow` exists in `jarvis/db/models.py` but is unused anywhere; `TranscriptStore` and `AudioBufferStore` are purely volatile in-memory structures with no audio replay or retry endpoints and no Inspection Panel in the React dashboard.

## 3. Baseline Empirical Evidence Observed in Repository
- **Existing English Benchmark (`benchmarks/stt_benchmark_results.json`, 7 clips)**:
  - `base` (`balanced`, 6 threads): load `0.96s`, mean latency `1.24s` (`RTF 0.687`), mean WER `19.7%` (`"Stop."` 0.59s failed 100% as `"Sup."`; `"nine hundred"` failed string vs digit normalization `"900"`).
  - `small` (`balanced`, 6 threads): load `2.66s`, mean latency `4.61s` (`RTF 3.041`), mean WER `17.1%` (`"Stop."` still failed 100% as `"SOP"` / `"SAP."`).
- **Diagnostic Re-run on `benchmarks/samples/*.wav` (`task-5504`, CPU `int8`)**:
  - `live_malayalam.wav` (`4.32s`, `peak=0.1736`, `rms=0.0165`): `small` (`auto`) detected `ml (0.929)` and emitted Devanagari `"निंदान सम्यएक..."`; after `0.7` peak normalization, `small` emitted `"विंदान समयः..."`; `base` (`auto`) detected `tl (0.148)` and emitted `"In dano sa meyak..."`.
  - `live_mixed.wav` (`9.36s`, `peak=0.0027`, `rms=0.00058`): un-normalized returned `""`; naive `0.7/peak` normalization without a gain cap or noise-floor check produced high-logprob hallucination loops (`"1-2-3-4-4..."`, `"වවව..."`, `"ༀༀ..."`).
  - `live_wake.wav` (`4.72s`, `peak=0.2104`, `rms=0.0243`): `base` (`auto`) transcribed `"Hey Jarvis, what's the time?"` accurately while falsely reporting language `mr (0.171)`.

## 4. Phase 4 Model Feasibility Note (Target Machine: Ryzen 5 5600H / GTX 1650 4 GB / 16 GB RAM / Python 3.14)
- **Active `.venv` runtime**: Python `3.14.6` with `faster-whisper 1.2.1`, `ctranslate2 4.9.0`, `onnxruntime 1.31.1`, `scipy 1.18.1`, `numpy 2.5.4`, `sounddevice 0.5.6`. Heavy `torch`/`transformers` packages are not installed in `.venv` (and GTX 1650 `4 GB` VRAM is largely occupied by Ollama `qwen3:4b-instruct`).
- **`sajilck/whisper-small-malayalam-v2`**: OpenAI `whisper-small` architecture (`244M` params, **Apache-2.0** license, reported `31.2%` WER / `4.0%` CER on Malayalam). Distributed on Hugging Face as PyTorch/Safetensors (`~968 MB`), requiring conversion to **CTranslate2 INT8** (`~250 MB` quantized, runnable inside `faster-whisper` on CPU with zero PyTorch runtime) or Hugging Face `transformers` inference.
- **`ai4bharat/indicconformer_stt_ml_hybrid_ctc_rnnt_large`** & 600M multilingual variants: Hybrid Conformer (`120M` params monolingual `ml`, **MIT** license). Distributed natively for NVIDIA NeMo/PyTorch, with community **ONNX INT8** exports usable via `onnxruntime` / `sherpa-onnx` without PyTorch.
- **Architectural Implication**: Implement a clean `ASRProvider` interface (`FasterWhisperProvider`, `MalayalamASRProvider`, `IndicConformerProvider`) that loads optional local CTranslate2/ONNX/Transformers checkpoints lazily when present under `models/asr/`, never downloads large models automatically on startup, enforces CPU-first `int8`/ONNX execution on this 4 GB VRAM budget, and falls back gracefully with actionable guidance when optional external weights are not yet installed.
